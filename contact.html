FAQ

Common questions

How do I request a tow?

Call MECHOTOW or send a WhatsApp message with your location, vehicle details and the problem. The team will then confirm availability and next steps with you.

Can you tow heavier vehicles?

Heavy-duty towing is offered for eligible vehicles, depending on the equipment available. Please share your vehicle type when you contact us.

Do you come to my home or roadside for repairs?

Yes, for suitable issues. Some problems may need a workshop, and the team will tell you what is possible.

Which areas do you cover?

Coverage depends on your location. Contact us with where you are and we will confirm whether we can help.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the service, distance and vehicle. We do not list fixed prices here, so please contact MECHOTOW for a quote.

Does sending a WhatsApp message book a service?

No. A message is an inquiry. A service is only arranged once the MECHOTOW team confirms availability and details with you.
