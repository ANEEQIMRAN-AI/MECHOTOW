MECHOTOW website
1. Open js/config.js and add your phone, WhatsApp (digits only, e.g. 923001234567), area, hours.
2. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN in sitemap.xml and robots.txt after you choose a domain.
3. Upload the whole folder to any static host, or open index.html to preview.

UPDATE: Service Areas
- Nav tab "Service Areas" opens a city list; each city has its own page (lahore.html, multan.html, ...).
- City images: assets/img/cities/
- Customer reviews: paste REAL reviews into js/config.js > reviews:[...]. They show on the homepage with the average and ranking.
